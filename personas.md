# User Personas & Contextual Stories

This document outlines the target user personas for the **Pomodoro Note-Taker & Scheduler** web application and provides real-world contextual stories demonstrating how the tool solves their specific workflow challenges.

## Persona 1: Alex – The Computer Science Student

### Profile

- **Role:** 2nd-Year University Student
- **Background:** Alex juggles multiple programming assignments, lectures, and group projects. They struggle with context-switching and frequently get distracted when hopping between calendar apps, note apps, and timer tools.
- **Goal:** Stay focused during long study sessions and track assignment deadlines in one place.

### Contextual Story

> _"Before finding this web app, my browser had at least 30 tabs open at any given moment. I had Google Calendar open in one tab, a separate Pomodoro site in another, and Notion open for taking notes. Every time the Pomodoro timer dinged, I would switch tabs to write down what I finished, only to get distracted by notifications or YouTube recommendations._
>
> _Using the Pomodoro Note-Taker app completely changed my study workflow. Now, I open a single page. I can schedule my study sessions for the week on the calendar, start my 25-minute Pomodoro timer, and take lecture notes right on the same screen. Having everything in one view keeps me focused and prevents me from getting lost in tab-switching."_

## Persona 2: Maya – The Remote Freelance Designer

### Profile

- **Role:** Freelance UX/UI Designer
- **Background:** Maya manages multiple client deliverables simultaneously. She needs a simple way to track time spent on specific tasks while making quick design logs and meeting notes.
- **Goal:** Maintain deep work intervals while easily logging progress and scheduled client meetings.

### Contextual Story

> _"As a freelancer, staying organized is everything. I used to waste so much time opening three different software applications just to kick off a 50-minute design session. I'd forget to write down notes from client feedback because my note app wasn't readily open next to my timer._
>
> _With this all-in-one web app, I schedule my client review meetings directly on the built-in calendar. When it’s time to design, I hit start on the Pomodoro timer and jot down ideas or quick feedback directly into the note editor. It gives me a clean, distraction-free dashboard where I can track my time and record my work simultaneously."_
